### What tools do people use to monitor industry trends and competitors?

People typically combine free or low-cost monitoring sources with paid platforms, choosing based on signal value and budget. [src8](https://github.com/ncklrs/startup-os-skills/blob/HEAD/skills/competitive-strategist/rules/monitoring-systems.md) Common free-to-low-cost sources include news and PR alerts, social media notifications, product release notes and review sites, which are often rated medium to high in signal value. [src8](https://github.com/ncklrs/startup-os-skills/blob/HEAD/skills/competitive-strategist/rules/monitoring-systems.md) Social platforms are also used to follow companies, industry leaders and relevant hashtags, and to watch hiring patterns and leadership changes as signals of how competitors are evolving. [src3](https://www.business.com/articles/14-simple-solutions-tracking-industrys-trends/?utm_campaign=06132022_Tuesday_Bbuilding%3Futm_campaign%3D06132022_Tuesday_Bbuilding%3Futm_campaign%3D06132022_Tuesday_Bbuilding%3Futm_campaign%3D06132022_Tuesday_Bbuilding) Paid or dedicated platforms carry higher cost and are generally rated high in signal value, with some commercial offerings priced around $15k–$60k per year. [src6](https://apify.com/noniner/competitor-pulse) [src8](https://github.com/ncklrs/startup-os-skills/blob/HEAD/skills/competitive-strategist/rules/monitoring-systems.md) Pay-as-you-go and API-first options exist at lower entry points, with examples priced from about $50 to $66 per 1,000 reports or monitored competitors. [src4](https://apify.com/apricot_blackberry/competitive-intelligence) [src6](https://apify.com/noniner/competitor-pulse) Systematic, automated monitoring tends to outperform sporadic manual research because it surfaces changes consistently and supports faster response. ### How can I tell if my investment thesis is still valid after new information?

要判断投资论点在新信息出现后是否仍然有效，核心做法是回到最初写下的论点本身逐条核对，而不是先看价格走势。 [src1](https://www.etoro.com/el/posts/f99e0810-73e3-11f1-8080-8001442e6b2b) [src4](https://stocksdownunder.com/questions-investors-need-to-ask/) 具体而言，应区分价格波动与论点有效性：价格下跌不一定意味着论点被推翻，价格上涨也不等于论点得到确认。 [src4](https://stocksdownunder.com/questions-investors-need-to-ask/) [src8](https://www.etoro.com/posts/89766cc0-a3af-11f1-8080-80003db3095a) 可以主动寻找能否定论点的证据，而非只搜集支持自己的信息，并据此检验原有假设是否仍然成立。 [src7](https://www.finanzen.net/ratgeber/aktien/grundlagen-einstieg/was-dir-niemand-ueber-aktien-recherche-erzaehlt-das-geheimnis-der-profis/net/) 同时应把投资论点当作一份持续更新的文件，用新信息修正它，但不能借机为情绪化持仓随意改写论点。 [src3](https://jasonapollovoss.com/web/wp-content/uploads/press/INTUIT_chapter.pdf) 若复核后发现原有论点已被证伪，较为一致的做法是卖出并转向其他机会，即使这意味着承担亏损。 [src5](http://www.fidelity.co.uk/pdfs/adviser/ab_thesis_behind_convictions.pdf)


### How do I avoid confirmation bias when following a company I already invested in?

同时，有意识地查找看空信息、了解反面观点的原因和逻辑，而不是只收集利好材料。 [src2](https://www.mangalore-icai.org/Attachment/163928721_The%20Oracle%20Newsletter%20-%20May_June%202024_compressed%20(4).pdf) 定期复盘也有帮助：记录每次买卖决策的理由，回看当时支持决策的信息是否全面、被忽略的反面证据后来是否成真。 [src5](https://edu.longone.com.cn/app?d=www&c=Zhengquanxueyuan&m=CourseDetails&id=12514) 此外，分散配置、把部分研究或决策环节交给外部机制，也是可考虑的缓解方式。 [src5](https://edu.longone.com.cn/app?d=www&c=Zhengquanxueyuan&m=CourseDetails&id=12514) 需要注意的是，仅仅了解偏差的存在并不足以让人免疫，仍需借助外部方法来持续校正判断。 [src6](https://thewealthybarber.com/video/confirmation-bias-be-careful-when-investing/)


### how to track all news about a stock and update my thesis

要跟踪个股新闻并更新投资论点，可先明确论点的各个支柱，再将每个支柱映射到可测量的数据点，然后定期拉取最新证据并逐项判定其状态。 [src2](https://github.com/aiskillstore/marketplace/blob/dbe0e719813583400773166a0621f8a9b8185c72/skills/longbridge/longbridge-research/references/thesis-tracker.md?plain=1) [src5](https://github.com/longbridge/skills/blob/main/skills/longbridge-research/references/thesis-tracker.md?plain=1) 具体做法是：为每个支柱标注“仍成立／已削弱／已破裂”，并附上支撑数据，最后给出整体置信度评分（高／中／低）与建议动作。 [src2](https://github.com/aiskillstore/marketplace/blob/dbe0e719813583400773166a0621f8a9b8185c72/skills/longbridge/longbridge-research/references/thesis-tracker.md?plain=1) [src5](https://github.com/longbridge/skills/blob/main/skills/longbridge-research/references/thesis-tracker.md?plain=1) 为控制更新频率，可设置确定性触发阈值，例如一周内出现 3 篇以上新文章、价格波动超过 2 倍 ATR、目标价修正超过 10%、财报前后 5 天、或 30 天内有 2 名以上内部人交易，仅对触发阈值的标的执行论点更新。 [src6](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nousergon/crucible-research/b94d5daacfdc842ee59fbe5b98bec2024a224922/agents/sector_teams/material_triggers.py) 新闻抓取后应先做清洗提取，将原始页面转为干净文本，再按输出模式做结构化研究，以减少噪声对判断的干扰。 [src4](https://parallel.ai/articles/how-to-set-up-continuous-web-monitoring-for-investment-research) 也可按固定周期（如过去 24 小时或 7 天）整理新闻，区分已确认事实、合理推论与待确认之处，并判断其对论点是强化、削弱还是暂无重大影响。 [src8](http://stocks.ddns.net/Forum/511/%e7%9c%8b%e7%9b%a4%e8%bb%9f%e9%ab%94.aspx?goto=15969)

### How do I avoid hindsight bias when checking whether I was right?

无法完全避免后见之明偏差，但可以通过记录原始判断、事后对照结果并区分当时可知信息与事后信息来降低其影响。 [src6](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aidanmccullen_innovation-decisionmaking-bias-activity-7240693422053896192-Ya9O) [src8](https://conservancy.umn.edu/items/8a6baf2a-656e-4191-a99f-24a81f6bad5f) 一种做法是在做出判断时写下当时的概率估计，事后先回忆该估计再核对结果，因为人们常会记成比实际更高的概率。 [src1](https://direct.mit.edu/books/oa-monograph-pdf/2558584/book_9780262384421.pdf?__cf_chl_tk=9ZEKwGD7qAx59niBRRkN3dSjP3KXrOT7O3a3JIvAnDA-1787852133-1.0.1.1-GDw3PEplFF3fiq6CKuTO6mF._dqOshw8Ld4smF7NOw8&_x_output_type_b6db407c4_=embedded_pdf) [src2](https://www.kevindorst.com/uploads/8/8/1/7/88177244/25.9.18_ch_hb.pdf) 实验表明，提供能区分“原始判断”与“正确答案”的检索线索，有助于选择性地回忆当初的判断。 [src8](https://conservancy.umn.edu/items/8a6baf2a-656e-4191-a99f-24a81f6bad5f) 另有研究显示，仅靠单次警告或一般性提示来消除这种偏差，效果有限。 [src7](https://thejuryexpert.com/wp-content/uploads//1308/JuryExpert_1308_HindsightBias.pdf) 此外，可将校准拆分为敏感性与偏差两个指标分别评估，即区分对错判断的置信度差异与平均置信度同实际准确率的差距。 [src5](https://scholarsbank.uoregon.edu/bitstreams/3812ca88-08c2-4b6d-929e-ec42df86e58f/download)


### How do I keep track of my predictions and check them later?

Record each prediction with a probability and a resolution date, then score it against the outcome once known. [src1](https://pypi.org/project/augur-cli/) [src3](https://apps.apple.com/sk/app/claimcheck-prediction-tracker/id6761207158) A common method is to log the forecast, set a deadline or reminder, and mark it resolved when the result is clear. [src3](https://apps.apple.com/sk/app/claimcheck-prediction-tracker/id6761207158) [src4](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.redheartstudio.myprediction.prod) Scoring can use proper rules such as the Brier score, log score, and a reliability diagram showing how often events assigned a given probability actually occurred. [src1](https://pypi.org/project/augur-cli/) Calibration improves with practice: among events you rated 90%, roughly nine in ten should happen for good calibration. [src5](https://blog.beeminder.com/fatebook/) Some tools count private as well as public predictions toward tracking goals. [src2](https://help.beeminder.com/article/366-fatebook) Implementations differ in tradeoffs: some are local and dependency-free, while others are hosted platforms or mobile apps with reminders and proof uploads. [src1](https://pypi.org/project/augur-cli/) [src3](https://apps.apple.com/sk/app/claimcheck-prediction-tracker/id6761207158) [src4](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.redheartstudio.myprediction.prod)


### How do I tell whether new information actually changes my forecast?

要判断新信息是否真正改变了预测，应比较信息到达前后对同一二元问题的置信度标量，并观察其是否朝参考标准的方向修正。 [src1](https://export.arxiv.org/pdf/2509.23936) 可将每次更新视为一次新观测，分别记录更新前的预测值与更新后的预测值，并列出该观测在各影响日期上的具体影响。 [src5](https://www.statsmodels.org/v0.14.4/examples/notebooks/generated/statespace_news.html) 在评估时，需同时记录预测置信度与不确定性，并说明哪些假设对预期结果影响最大。 [src2](https://ones.com/blog/checklist-for-deciding-when-repeated-software-project-estimate-misses-require-a-cost-forecast-rebaseline-a-practical-guide-for-2026/) 若以准确度计分，按问题开放期间逐日平均的评分方式会促使预测者在新信息出现时及时更新。 [src7](https://goodjudgment.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Forecasters-Who-Think-Again-Are-More-Accurate.pdf) 此外，用自然频率而非概率格式呈现信息，可能帮助人们更准确地修正先验概率。 [src6](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0148296315001411)

### What's the best way to compare what I thought would happen with what actually happened?

最稳妥的做法是在每次运行前先记录预期结果，运行后再与观测结果逐项比对，而不是事后凭记忆判断。 [src5](https://community.n8n.io/t/testing-the-green-execution-that-quietly-produces-no-output/324764/10) [src8](https://community.n8n.io/t/testing-the-green-execution-that-quietly-produces-no-output/324764/8) 比对时应把预期与观测分开保存，并记录上游预期数量、实际处理数量和最终结果及原因，以便区分“预期为零”和“预期非零却为零”两种情况。 [src8](https://community.n8n.io/t/testing-the-green-execution-that-quietly-produces-no-output/324764/8) 若某一步报告的结果与其事先声明不符，可以收集差异并继续执行，由调用方决定差异的价值；但若某一步抛出异常，则应停止运行，因为无法判断后续步骤。 [src1](https://pub.dev/documentation/modular_cli_sdk/latest/testing/PreviewExecutor/perform.html) 当输入本身无法给出预期数量时，应借助上游响应或后续核对检查作为证据，而不是默认把零视为有效。 [src5](https://community.n8n.io/t/testing-the-green-execution-that-quietly-produces-no-output/324764/10) 对于看似正常却无输出的情况，可先写清预期再运行，并考虑用外部系统的总量作为独立参照。 [src5](https://community.n8n.io/t/testing-the-green-execution-that-quietly-produces-no-output/324764/10) [src8](https://community.n8n.io/t/testing-the-green-execution-that-quietly-produces-no-output/324764/8)
